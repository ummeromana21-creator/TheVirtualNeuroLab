Data & citation. Each fly's region numbers come only from its own scan, exported from FlyWire Codex: Maxime = BANC (Bates, Phelps et al., Nature 2026), Hagrid = male CNS (Berg et al., Cell 2026); only neuron pairs joined by 5+ synapses are counted, the same way for both. The two scans were made by different labs, so don't read their numbers as a sex difference. Region names and families follow Ito et al. 2014 and Court et al. 2020. 3D pictures and cell-type counts use the FlyWire reference brain (FAFB v783; Dorkenwald et al. 2024, Schlegel et al. 2024). Cite Codex: doi:10.13140/RG.2.2.35928.67844; FlyWire papers per their citation table; use subject to the FlyWire Terms. Nerve-cord pictures show each fly's own measured cell bodies (BANC v888 release; male CNS v1.0 release).
